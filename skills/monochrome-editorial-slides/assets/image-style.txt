Use realistic architectural or subject-relevant photographic details with strong geometry, precise natural textures, calm daylight and uncluttered composition. Photographs sit within clean rectangular crops and do not blend into the slide background. Subjects may be architecture and space, people collaborating (hands, backs, profiles, small groups), objects and material details, or city and natural light; keep one consistent cool, low-saturation grade across the deck.
